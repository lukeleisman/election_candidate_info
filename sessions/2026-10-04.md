### Prior work found
- `southsideweekly/ald_finance` (R, last push 2019-02-18): pulled 2019 aldermanic receipts from
  `http://illinoissunshine.org/api/receipts/?committee_id=…&received_date__ge=…&datatype=csv`.
  Committee IDs came from a "TDL - Aldermanic Spreadsheet" (The Daily Line). Cycle start was "day after
  last municipal election" (2015-02-26). Did donor-name/org cleaning + industry coding.
- `shu1513/electionssimplified` — `docs/plans/plan-illinois-local-finance.md` (written 2026-07-11, repo
  pushed 2026-10-04). Its audit of its own ISBE pipeline lists pitfalls: matching committees by
  guessing from names (should use the official SBE link); itemized sums labeled as total receipts (SBE
  doesn't itemize ≤ $150; D-2 has true totals); transfers/loans/in-kind counted as contributions;
  double counting across a candidate's multiple committees; approximate cycle windows; the live SBE
  export was unreliable, so it uses the bulk files.
